Reading this: each published strain is drawn with its Table S1 ecological/geographic cluster's color and marker shape. Our three isolates (YH26, YH72, YH140) are nearly coincident in PC space — genome-wide distances between them are an order of magnitude smaller than to any published strain — so they're shown as a single black-diamond group rather than three overlapping points. In the zoomed panel, our strains sit closest to Canada-trees along PC1, offset slightly on PC2 from the main core cluster — consistent with the ADMIXTURE and tree placements, and with a wild population that has diverged somewhat from the specific published Canada-trees accessions.
