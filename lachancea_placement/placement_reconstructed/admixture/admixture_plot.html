Supervised ADMIXTURE (K=6): our 3 strains vs. the 145-strain published panel

Each bar is one sample's estimated ancestry proportions across the six published ecological/geographic clusters, with cluster identity fixed from Table S1 as ADMIXTURE's supervised reference labels — a model-based placement, independent of the PCA and BIONJ-tree cross-checks. Samples are grouped by assigned cluster; hover a bar for exact proportions.

Reading this: nearly every published strain resolves to >99.9% membership in its own labeled cluster — expected for a supervised run and a good sanity check that the reconstructed panel and reference labels line up correctly. The two visibly mixed bars under Unknown are the two Table S1 gap samples (no published cluster assignment); one splits Europe/Domestic-2 vs. Europe-Mix ancestry, the other is admixed across most clusters. All three of our strains are essentially pure Canada-trees (99.995% each, marked with a black tick above their bars) — the same placement independently reached by both PCA and the BIONJ tree.
